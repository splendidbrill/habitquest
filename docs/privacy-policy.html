HabitQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026 · App: HabitQuest (habitquest.uk / uk.habitquest.app)

HabitQuest is a family health app. Parents plan meals and exercise, and children complete daily missions and build streaks. Because children use this app, we have deliberately kept the amount of information we collect small, and we do not show advertising or sell data to anyone.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you remove it.

1. Who we are

HabitQuest is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: habitquest247@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information a parent provides

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create and secure the parent account. Passwords are stored hashed; we never see them.
Family and child profiles — first name or nickname, age or age band, avatar, goalsTo tailor missions, meals and exercises to the right age group.

We ask only for a first name or nickname for a child. Please do not enter a full name, school, address or any other identifying detail — none of it is needed.

Information created by using the app

DataWhy
Mission and check-in history, streaks, points, badges and titlesTo show progress and keep streaks accurate.
Meal choices, feedback and preferencesTo improve meal suggestions for your family.
Photos you upload of meals and activitiesOnly to display them in the app’s photo rewards feature.
Which in-app videos were watched, and for how longTo understand which guidance is useful.
Notification tokensTo send reminders to your device.
Bug reports and feedback you send usTo fix problems you report.

What we do not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone or browsing history.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Photos

Photos are uploaded only when you deliberately choose them. The app uses your device’s own photo picker, so it never browses your gallery — it receives only the single picture you select. Photos are stored privately and are visible only inside your own family account. We do not analyse them, use them for training, or share them with anyone.

4. Children’s privacy

HabitQuest is designed for families with children, and we treat children’s information with particular care:

  • A parent or guardian creates the account. Children do not register, and there is no login that a child can create alone.
  • Children’s profiles are visible only within the family account.
  • There is no advertising, no in-app purchasing, and no behavioural profiling of children.
  • There is no chat, messaging, or social feature. Children cannot communicate with anyone through the app, and nothing a child does is published or shared outside the family account.
  • We collect the minimum needed for the app to work, and never sell or share children’s data with third parties for their own purposes.

If you believe a child has provided us with information without a parent’s involvement, email habitquest247@gmail.com and we will delete it.

5. Legal basis (UK / EU users)

  • Contract — to provide the app you asked for: your account, plans and progress.
  • Consent — for notifications, camera and photo access. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure and fixing faults.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of providers purely to run the service:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, login and photo storage
Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification serviceDelivering reminder notifications

These providers process data on our instructions only. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Where data is stored, and for how long

Data is stored on Supabase servers and protected by row-level security, so one family’s data cannot be read by another. Data is encrypted in transit.

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything is erased immediately; encrypted backups may retain a copy for up to 30 days before rotating out, and are never used to restore a deleted account.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, and object to how we use it. To exercise any of these, email habitquest247@gmail.com. UK and EU users may also complain to their data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all its data at any time from inside the app — Delete my account on the profile selector or parent home screen — or by request. Full instructions: Delete your HabitQuest account and data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
