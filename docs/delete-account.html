Delete your HabitQuest account and data

App: HabitQuest (com.habitquest) · Developer: HabitQuest

You can permanently delete your HabitQuest account and everything stored with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open HabitQuest and sign in.
  2. Go to the profile selector or the Parent Home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are erased immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send an email to habitquest247@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my HabitQuest account”.

We verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days, and confirm by email when it is done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account and login credentialsPermanently deleted
Parent profile and family settingsPermanently deleted
Child profiles (name, age, avatar, goals)Permanently deleted
Meal plans, grocery lists and recipesPermanently deleted
Exercise plans, missions and challengesPermanently deleted
Streaks, points, badges and progress historyPermanently deleted
Feedback and bug reports you submittedPermanently deleted
Push notification tokensPermanently deleted

What we keep, and for how long

  • Nothing personal is retained. Deletion removes all data linked to your account.
  • Encrypted database backups may still contain your data for up to 30 days, after which they are rotated out automatically. Backups are never used to restore a deleted account.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage counts that cannot identify you or your children may be kept for product analytics.

Deleting a single child instead of the whole account

If you only want to remove one child, open Parent Home, select that child, and remove their profile there. That deletes their plans and progress while keeping your account active.
